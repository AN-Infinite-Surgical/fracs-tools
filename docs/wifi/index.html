WWound
W0
No ulcer, no gangrene. Ischaemic rest pain (typical symptoms plus ischaemia grade 3).
W1
Small, shallow ulcer on distal leg or foot; no exposed bone unless limited to distal phalanx. No gangrene. Salvageable with 1–2 digit amputation or skin cover.
W2
Deeper ulcer with exposed bone, joint or tendon, generally not the heel; or shallow heel ulcer without calcaneal involvement. Gangrene limited to digits. Salvageable with 3 or more digit amputations or standard TMA.
W3
Extensive deep ulcer of forefoot and/or midfoot; or deep, full-thickness heel ulcer with or without calcaneal involvement. Extensive forefoot/midfoot gangrene or full-thickness heel necrosis. Needs complex reconstruction (non-traditional TMA, Chopart, Lisfranc) or flap cover.

fIFoot infection
fI0
No symptoms or signs of infection.
fI1
Two or more of: local swelling or induration; erythema >0.5 to 2 cm around the ulcer; local tenderness or pain; local warmth; purulent discharge. Skin and subcutaneous tissue only, no systemic signs. Exclude trauma, gout, acute Charcot, fracture, thrombosis, venous stasis.
fI2
Local infection with erythema >2 cm, or involving deeper structures (abscess, osteomyelitis, septic arthritis, fasciitis). No SIRS.
fI3
Local infection with two or more SIRS signs: temperature >38 °C or <36 °C; HR >90; RR >20 or PaCO₂ <32 mmHg; WCC >12,000 or <4,000/mm³ or 10% bands.

Matches IDSA/PEDIS uninfected, mild, moderate, severe. GVG 2019 Table 3.4
