Sources and caveats

One point each for age >76 years, pH <7.2, creatinine >2.0 mg/dL (>177 µmol/L) and systolic BP <70 mmHg at any time before repair. Garland BT et al. Preoperative risk score for the prediction of mortality after repair of ruptured abdominal aortic aneurysms. J Vasc Surg 2018;68:991–7 (n=303, Harborview 2002–2013; erratum J Vasc Surg 2018;68:1623).

Derivation, all repairs, 30 days: 1 point 22%, 2 points 69%, 3 points 80%, 4 points 100% (Garland abstract). Hemingway 2021 restates 3 points as 78%. The abstract does not report 0 points.

Validation, modern era Nov 2013–Dec 2018, 106 repairs: 0 points 14.6%, 1 point 35.7%, 2 points 68.4%, 3 or 4 points 100%. After adjustment for the score, 30-day mortality was 41.3% after open repair and 31.6% after EVAR (P = .2). Hemingway JF et al. J Vasc Surg 2021;74:1508–18.

ESVS 2024 AAA guidelines §6.2.6: the Harborview score relies only on preoperative variables and may help discussions with referring physicians, patients and families; withholding treatment or choosing palliation on a score alone is not recommended. In an independent community dataset (n=38) its AUC was 0.792 overall and 0.893 for rEVAR (Hansen 2019).
