Sources and caveats

Scoring bands: Copeland GP, Jones D, Walters M. POSSUM: a scoring system for surgical audit. Br J Surg 1991;78:355–60. Each of 12 physiological and 6 operative variables scores 1, 2, 4 or 8; a variable with no data scores 1 (as applied in P-POSSUM studies, e.g. Saudi J Anaesth 2019;13:46–51). Physiological score 12–88, operative severity score 6–48.

POSSUM: ln[R/(1−R)] = −5.91 + 0.16·PS + 0.19·OS (morbidity); −7.04 + 0.13·PS + 0.16·OS (mortality). P-POSSUM: −9.065 + 0.1692·PS + 0.1550·OS (Prytherch DR et al. Br J Surg 1998;85:1217–20). POSSUM over-predicted overall mortality more than twofold, and more than sevenfold in patients at ≤5% risk; P-POSSUM fitted observed in-hospital mortality closely.

V-POSSUM, major arterial surgery (Prytherch DR et al. Eur J Vasc Endovasc Surg 2001;21:477–83): full −8.0616 + 0.1552·PS + 0.1238·OS; physiology only −6.0386 + 0.1539·PS. RAAA-POSSUM, ruptured AAA: full −4.9795 + 0.0913·PS + 0.0958·OS; physiology only −2.7569 + 0.0968·PS. Equations as tabulated by Neary WD, Heather BP, Earnshaw JJ. Br J Surg 2003;90:157–65. The physiology-only forms need no operative data and can be used before surgery.

Rutherford 11e Ch 69: none of the rAAA scores (Hardman, Glasgow, V- and RAAA-POSSUM, Edinburgh, Vancouver, VSGNE) predicts outcome accurately enough to deny repair. POSSUM was devised for surgical audit and case-mix comparison (Copeland 1991; Neary 2003).

Definitions

Copeland 1991. Haemoglobin in g/dL, white cell count ×10⁹/L, urea mmol/L.
